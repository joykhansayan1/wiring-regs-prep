Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026

Summary: This app does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. All of your study data stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. 18th Edition Wiring Regs Prep does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or any other personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your study progress — such as answered questions, scores, streaks, bookmarks, flashcard schedules, your chosen exam date and daily goal — is stored locally on your device only. It is never uploaded to us or to any third party. Deleting the app removes this data.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store (iOS) or by Google through Google Play Billing (Android). We never see or store your payment details. Apple’s or Google’s handling of your purchase is governed by their own privacy policies. The Android app only reads your active purchases from Google Play to unlock access; this is not shared with us.

Notifications

If you enable the optional daily study reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No notification data leaves your device, and you can turn reminders off at any time in Settings.

Children’s privacy

The app is intended for adults preparing for a professional qualification and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
