Pass the 18th Edition exam with confidence.

An independent study app for electricians preparing for the City & Guilds 2382 (18th Edition) Wiring Regulations exam. Over 2,000 original practice questions, full-length timed mock exams, flashcards with spaced repetition, and a readiness dashboard that shows exactly where you stand.

What’s inside

  • 2,000+ original exam-style questions across every topic area
  • Full-length timed mock exams (open-book style, 60 questions / 2 hours)
  • Quick practice and practice-by-topic
  • Flashcards with spaced repetition
  • Review of incorrect and bookmarked questions
  • Readiness score, per-topic mastery, streaks and exam countdown
  • Fully offline — no account, no internet needed to study

Available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store and for Android on Google Play. Full access requires a subscription or one-time purchase (see the app for current pricing). A 3-day free trial is available on the yearly plan.
